Nightmare Tag is a thrilling VR horror game that takes place in your nightmares! Scare your friends as a monster with abilities designed to hunt, or survive as a human in intense PvP & PvE game modes. Decide fight or flight quickly! Fight back with a modifiable slingshot, or break line of sight and stand completely still to go invisible! Play for free as Antonio or the Entity. Buy General Admission to unlock the full game with more characters, upgradable perks, and PvE lobby hosting.
